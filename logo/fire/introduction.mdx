The Niagara Fire Department responds to fires, medical calls, motor vehicle collisions, and rescues across the Niagara response area. Members work alongside OPP units on shared scenes, so familiarity with radio procedure matters as much as fireground skill.

<Info>
New members should read this page, then [Apparatus and Callsigns](/fire/apparatus), then [Response Types](/fire/response) before signing on to a shift.
</Info>

## Ranks

| Rank | Role |
| ---- | ---- |
| Probationary Firefighter | New member under supervision, cannot ride alone |
| Firefighter | Full member, cleared for independent assignment |
| Lieutenant | Leads a single apparatus and its crew |
| Captain | Commands a station, supervises multiple crews |
| Chief | Overall command authority, sets policy |

## Expectations

<AccordionGroup>

<Accordion title="Sign on and off with dispatch" icon="radio">
Every shift starts and ends with dispatch. Nobody rides unassigned, and dispatch cannot send you what it does not know you have.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Scene safety comes before the task" icon="hard-hat">
Full gear on every call. Nobody enters a structure alone, and no member operates outside their training level.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Command is single and clear" icon="users">
The first arriving officer establishes command and holds it until formally transferring it. Announce the transfer over the air.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Work with OPP, not around them" icon="shield">
On shared scenes, police own traffic and the perimeter. Coordinate through dispatch rather than freelancing.
</Accordion>

</AccordionGroup>
