Fire callsigns identify the apparatus, not the person riding it. When a member changes trucks, the callsign changes with the truck.

## Callsign structure

Apparatus type followed by station number, spoken as written.

| Callsign | Reads as | Unit |
| -------- | -------- | ---- |
| E1 | Engine One | Engine at Station 1 |
| L2 | Ladder Two | Ladder at Station 2 |
| R1 | Rescue One | Heavy rescue at Station 1 |
| T3 | Tanker Three | Water tender at Station 3 |
| M2 | Medic Two | Ambulance at Station 2 |
| BC1 | Battalion One | Chief officer |

## Apparatus roles

<Tabs>

<Tab title="Engine">

The primary fire attack unit. Carries hose, water, and pump. First due on structure fires and handles the initial line.

Minimum crew of two. Officer rides front right and directs the attack.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Ladder">

Aerial apparatus for elevated attack, roof access, and rescue above ground level. Also carries the bulk of forcible entry and ventilation tools.

Requested by name when a building is more than two storeys or when roof operations are needed.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Rescue">

Extrication and technical rescue. Carries hydraulic tools for motor vehicle collisions, plus rope and confined space equipment.

Dispatch sends rescue automatically on any collision reported with entrapment.

</Tab>

<Tab title="Medic">

Patient care and transport. Attends all medical calls and stages at fire scenes for member rehabilitation and civilian injuries.

Stages a safe distance out on any call with an active hazard and waits to be called in.

</Tab>

</Tabs>

## On the air

<Steps>

<Step title="Identify by apparatus">
"Dispatch, Engine One." Never use a member name as a callsign.
</Step>

<Step title="Mark your status">
Responding, on scene, and available all go to dispatch as they happen.
</Step>

<Step title="Spell anything ambiguous">
Street names and cross streets go out in phonetics. See the [Phonetic Alphabet](/procedures/phonetic-alphabet) reference.
</Step>

</Steps>

<Note>
Station numbers and apparatus roster are a template. Replace with your actual fleet.
</Note>
